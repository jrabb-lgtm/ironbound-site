What we recover

Billions of dollars sit unclaimed because no one knows to ask for them.

Accounts get forgotten, owners move, and people pass away before their affairs are settled. When that happens, the money is often held for years by an institution that is waiting for the right person to come forward.

Estates and heirs

Money left in a loved one's name after they died. We identify the rightful heirs and open or reopen the estate so it can be paid.

Unclaimed accounts

Dormant bank balances, uncashed checks, stock and insurance proceeds that were turned over after the owner couldn't be reached.

Surplus from sales

Money left over after a property was sold to pay a debt, which belongs to the former owner or their family.

How it works

Three steps, and we do the heavy lifting.

Questions

What people usually ask us.

Is this legitimate?

Yes. We are a private Massachusetts company. We never ask for money up front, and we never ask for a bank password or card number. Everything we agree on is put in writing for you to review, and you're welcome to have your own attorney look it over.

Are you a government agency?

No. Ironbound Asset Recovery is an independent company. We are not affiliated with any state, court or government office.

Why don't you tell me where the money is right away?

Finding the money and proving who it belongs to takes real research. We share the details once we have an agreement, and we explain every step from there.

What if the person it belongs to has passed away?

That is most of what we do. We work out who the legal heirs are, and our attorneys handle the probate needed to release the money. You don't pay those legal fees.

What does it cost?

Nothing unless we succeed. Our fee is a percentage of what is recovered, agreed in writing in advance.
